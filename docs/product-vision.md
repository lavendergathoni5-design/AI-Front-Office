# 1. Executive Summary

## Overview

Virexa AI Front Office is an AI-powered virtual receptionist and lead management assistant designed specifically for real estate agencies.

The product helps agencies respond to customer inquiries instantly, qualify potential buyers, schedule property viewings, answer common questions, and automatically hand qualified leads to real estate agents.

The objective is to reduce missed opportunities, improve customer experience, and increase property sales through AI automation.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Real estate agencies face several operational challenges:

- Slow response to new customer inquiries.
- Missed opportunities due to delayed follow-up.
- Repetitive customer questions.
- Manual appointment scheduling.
- Poor organization of customer information.
- Excessive administrative work for agents.

These problems reduce productivity and lead to lost revenue.

---

# 3. Vision

To become the AI-powered front office that enables every real estate agency to deliver exceptional customer service while automating repetitive business processes.

---

# 4. Mission

Help real estate businesses automate customer communication, qualify leads, schedule appointments, and improve operational efficiency using practical AI.

---

# 5. Goals

The product should:

- Respond to customers within seconds.
- Capture every lead.
- Qualify potential buyers automatically.
- Book property viewings.
- Reduce repetitive work for agents.
- Improve customer satisfaction.
- Increase conversion from inquiry to viewing.

---

# 6. Success Metrics (KPIs)

The MVP will be considered successful if it achieves:

- Average response time below 10 seconds.
- 100% lead capture.
- Significant reduction in missed follow-ups.
- Increase in booked property viewings.
- High customer satisfaction.
- Improved lead conversion rate.

# 18. Risks

Potential risks:

- Incorrect property information
- AI hallucinations
- Outdated listings
- Poor internet connection
- Customer distrust of AI

Mitigation:

- Use verified data sources.
- Escalate uncertain questions.
- Regularly update listings.
- Log conversations for review.
